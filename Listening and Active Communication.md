# Listening and Active Communication

---

## 1. Active Listening

### Q1. What are the steps/strategies to do Active Listening?
* **Give full attention:** Focus completely on the speaker without distractions.
* **Maintain eye contact & body language:** Nod and look at the speaker to show engagement.
* **Wait your turn:** Do not interrupt while someone is speaking.
* **Check understanding:** Ask clarifying questions and repeat key points in your own words.
* **Stay open:** Avoid judging the speaker and take notes when necessary.

---

## 2. Reflective Listening

### Q2. According to Fisher's model, what are the key points of Reflective Listening?
Reflective listening means listening carefully and repeating or rephrasing the message to check whether it was understood correctly.

1. **Repeating:** Re-stating key words used by the speaker.
2. **Paraphrasing:** Explaining the main message in your own words.
3. **Reflecting:** Recognizing and expressing the speaker's feelings.

---

## 3. Reflection

### Q3. What are the obstacles in your listening process?
* Losing concentration or getting distracted by phone notifications.
* Thinking about your reply instead of listening.
* Making assumptions or jumping to conclusions quickly.
* Getting confused by difficult words.
* Interrupting others before they finish.

### Q4. What can you do to improve your listening?
* Put your phone away during conversations.
* Practice patience and listen fully before speaking.
* Ask questions whenever something is unclear.
* Take notes during meetings or key discussions.
* Practice active listening habits daily.

---

## 4. Types of Communication

### Q5. When do you switch to Passive communication style in your day-to-day life?
Passive communication means holding back your thoughts, staying silent, or yielding to others to avoid conflict.

* Talking to seniors or more experienced people.
* Fearing you might hurt someone's feelings.
* Wanting to avoid arguments.
* Feeling uncertain or lacking confidence in your opinion.

---

### Q6. When do you switch into Aggressive communication styles in your day-to-day life?
Aggressive communication means expressing your feelings or opinions forcefully, loudly, or harshly without respecting others.

* Being repeatedly interrupted.
* Feeling frustrated that someone is not understanding your point.
* Feeling angry or losing control of your emotions.
* Being blamed for mistakes unfairly.

---

### Q7. When do you switch into Passive-Aggressive communication styles in your day-to-day life?
Passive-aggressive communication means expressing anger or frustration indirectly using sarcasm, subtle hints, or silent treatment rather than direct conversation.

* Feeling ignored or unappreciated.
* Harboring anger without expressing it directly.
* Dealing with someone who repeatedly makes the same mistake.
* Using sarcasm instead of explaining true feelings.

---

### Q8. How can you make your communication assertive?
Assertive communication means expressing thoughts, feelings, and needs clearly and confidently while still respecting others.

* Speak calmly and confidently.
* Use polite and respectful language.
* State feelings clearly using direct statements.
* Learn to say "no" firmly when necessary.
* Control anger and respect other people's opinions.
* Listen carefully and accept constructive feedback.
