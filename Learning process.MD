# Video Notes and My Learning Reflections

### 1. What is the Feynman Technique?
* Basically, it's when you try to explain a hard topic in your own super simple words, like you're explaining it to someone who has zero clue about it.

---

### 2. What was the most interesting story or idea from the video?
* Honestly, I found the story about Salvador Dali and Thomas Edison super cool. They used to hold keys or small metal balls in their hands while relaxing in a chair. Right when they started dozing off, the item would drop, hit the floor, and wake them up. That way, they could grab all those weird, creative ideas popping into their heads in that middle zone between sleeping and being awake.

---

### 3. What are active and diffused modes of thinking?
* **Active mode:** This is when we are staring straight at a problem and trying *really* hard to solve it. Our brain stays focused on things we already know, kind of like a pinball bouncing fast between bumpers packed super close together.
* **Diffused mode:** This happens when we step back, relax, and stop forcing it. Our brain keeps working in the background and makes random new connections. It's like a pinball machine where the bumpers are spaced far apart, letting the ball wander around freely.

---

### 4. Steps to take when learning a new topic
1. Break down the big skill into smaller, bite-sized pieces.
2. Learn just enough so you can catch your own mistakes and correct them.
3. Remove any distractions or roadblocks that make starting/practicing hard.
4. Put in at least 20 hours of focused practice.
